Privacy Policy

Claude Personal Assistant · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Claude Personal Assistant is a private tool used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app has read-only access to:

  • Gmail messages in the owner's accounts (sender, subject and content of new messages).
  • Google Calendar events in the owner's accounts.

The app cannot send, delete or modify mail or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose: to prepare a daily task list for the owner. New messages and upcoming events are analysed to find the items that need the owner's attention, and a short summary of those items is sent to the owner.

Storage

Access tokens and the task list are stored only on the owner's own computer. Message content is not kept after it has been analysed, apart from the short task summaries.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone. To prepare the task list, message text is processed by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) on the owner's behalf, under the owner's own account with that service. The finished task list is delivered to the owner through the owner's own private messaging account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer removes everything it has stored.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the app's Google consent screen.
