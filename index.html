Claude Personal Assistant

A private, single-user assistant that turns incoming mail and calendar events into a daily task list.

What it does

Claude Personal Assistant is a personal tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. A few times a day it reads the owner's new Gmail messages and upcoming Google Calendar events, picks out the items that need attention, and sends the owner a short checklist.

Who can use it

The app is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up.

Google data

The app asks for read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It never sends, deletes or changes mail or calendar events. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
